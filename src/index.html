Privacy

Echo Board is offline-only. It is blocked from connecting to the internet: no accounts, no tracking, no cloud. Your sounds, voices, recordings and settings stay on this computer. Links you click (like vb-cable.com) open in your own browser.

Set up voice chat

Discord and games only listen to a microphone. Echo Board sends your voice and your sounds into a virtual microphone, and you pick that microphone in Discord.

Echo Board can install one for you: VB-CABLE, a free virtual audio cable by VB-Audio (included with Echo Board).

  1. Click Install virtual cable.
  2. Windows asks to allow changes: click Yes.
  3. Windows asks to install the VB-Audio driver: click Install.
  4. Restart your PC when it's done.
  1. Click Install virtual cable.
  2. Enter your Mac password when asked.
  3. Done. No restart needed.

Then in Discord

User Settings › Voice & Video › Input Device: choose CABLE Output, and set Output Device to your headphones. Turn off Krisp / noise suppression and echo cancellation. In games, pick the same device as your microphone.

VB-CABLE is made by VB-Audio (vb-cable.com). VB-CABLE is a donationware, all participations are welcome. You can donate on their site.

Already use BlackHole or VoiceMeeter? Pick it under Settings › Send to instead.

Help

Voice chat setup

Click No virtual cable at the top to install VB-CABLE. Then in Discord set Input Device to CABLE Output (Windows) or VB-Cable (Mac), and turn off noise suppression and echo cancellation. Keep Echo Board open while you play.

Pages and profiles

Each profile has 8 pages of 64 pads. Switch pages with the tabs above the grid or the Launchpad's top row of buttons. Drag a pad onto a tab to move it to that page. Switch whole layouts with the profile button next to the logo.

Voice changer

Click Voice at the top to pick an effect (Deep, Robot, Radio…). Toggle it from the right-side Launchpad buttons or a keyboard shortcut.

Instant replay

Turn it on in Settings. Echo Board keeps the last 15–60 seconds in memory only. Press Save replay (top bar, the top-right round Launchpad button, or a shortcut) and the clip lands on an empty pad. Use Trim in the pad editor to cut it down.

Text to speech

Type in the box under the pads and press Enter. Free AI voices are built in and run on your computer.

Default Launchpad buttons

  • Top row (1–8): pages
  • Right column, top: save replay · voice effect on/off · next effect
  • Right column, bottom: stop all sounds

Change these in Settings › Buttons & shortcuts. Esc in the app and Ctrl+Shift+K (Cmd+Shift+K on Mac) anywhere stop all sounds.

Privacy

Echo Board can't connect to the internet. Everything stays on your computer.
